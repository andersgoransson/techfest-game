How to score & win

Victory condition

Fill all six pillars to 80 or above to achieve a victory. Every pillar must reach the win threshold — protect your cloud architecture and win by driving every area past 80.

Points & pillar fills

  • Shooting a threat files it into its pillar: +8 fill to that pillar.
  • Your score equals the total of all pillar values multiplied by your current combo multiplier.

Combo multiplier

Each consecutive successful hit adds +1 to your combo multiplier — chain shots together for a higher score. Any breach resets the combo multiplier back to 1, so keep threats from getting through!

Breaches (threats reaching the bottom)

  • The breaching threat's pillar loses -6 fill.
  • Your cloud health drops by -10 per breach.
  • Cloud health at zero means game over.
  • Breach resets your combo multiplier to 1.

The six pillars

  • Operational Excellence
  • Security
  • Reliability
  • Performance Efficiency
  • Cost Optimization
  • Sustainability
